About

A curated layer for serious opportunities.

ProIdeaStore is where mature ideas become readable opportunity dossiers. The homepage shows the live dossier catalog; this page explains the portal.

What belongs here

  • Ideas with a clear buyer or user.
  • Evidence, risk notes, and missing diligence.
  • Prototype scope and asset lists.
  • Contributor history from earlier idea work.

What does not belong here

  • One-line gimmick ideas.
  • Fake investment readiness.
  • Unresearched claims.
  • Pitch language without evidence.

What gets refined

  • Customer and buying trigger.
  • Market substitutes and competitors.
  • Build path and MVP shape.
  • Risks that could stop the opportunity.

Where it can go

  • Investor or builder discussion.
  • Paid diligence work.
  • Prototype development.
  • Graduation into other Open Frontier stores.
