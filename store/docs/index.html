ProIdeaStore guide.

ProIdeaStore is the curated opportunity layer. Each page should help a builder, investor, or operator understand what is known and what still needs work.

Home is for browsing live dossiers. About and Docs are for explanation.

Dossier contents

  • Title and summary.
  • Buyer or target user.
  • Evidence already collected.
  • Missing diligence.
  • Assets such as prototype, memo, deck, or risk review.

Readiness

Readiness is not a vanity score. It should reflect clarity of customer, quality of evidence, risk honesty, and how obvious the next build step is.

Contribute well

  1. Use the console to add serious dossiers.
  2. Keep claims source-backed.
  3. Prefer explicit missing diligence over optimistic filler.
  4. Attach contributor work to real signed-in profiles.

Graduation

A strong dossier can move toward a prototype, pitch deck, paid research packet, or build inside another Open Frontier store.
